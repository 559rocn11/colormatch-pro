Privacy Policy — ColorMatch Pro

Effective date: November 19, 2026
Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers ColorMatch Pro (colormatch-pro.559rocn.workers.dev), a beauty shade and dupe matcher operated by Roc, doing business as ROCN / ReelyROCN, a sole proprietor based in Fresno, California.

Plain version: we collect as little as we can, we do not sell your information, and we do not use advertising trackers. This page explains exactly what we do collect, why, and who else touches it.

1. Information we collect

  • Your camera image: when you scan, the app processes your camera image on-device, in your browser, to measure your skin tone. This policy makes no claim about how long a photo is kept, because that behavior is not documented here.
  • Your scan result: the numeric result of a scan (skin-tone values such as lightness and undertone, lighting, and confidence — not your photo) is sent to our server and saved with your anonymous profile ID so your matches can improve over time.
  • Your profile ID and saved shades: the app creates an anonymous ID for your device and stores it, along with shades you save, in your own browser (localStorage) on your device.
  • No accounts, no payments: ColorMatch Pro has no accounts and takes no payments, so we do not collect names, email addresses, or payment details.

2. How we use your information

We use the information above only to:

  • Match you to shades and dupes.
  • Remember your profile and saved shades.
  • Reply to your messages.
  • Fix bugs and improve the app.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it for advertising. We do not use it to build profiles about you.

3. Third parties that receive information

When you use the app, these services receive some of your information directly — usually your IP address, browser information, and whatever you send them:

  • The stores you tap through to — when you tap a shade’s store link, you leave ColorMatch Pro and that store’s privacy policy applies. Some store links are affiliate links, which means the store may know you arrived from ColorMatch Pro.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy. We are not responsible for how they handle information once it reaches them.

4. Cookies and local storage

Our own servers do not set first-party cookies. The app uses your browser’s local storage to save your anonymous profile ID and your saved shades. That information stays on your device. Third-party embeds may set their own cookies when they load. You can clear cookies and local storage at any time in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep your information

We keep your information only as long as it is needed to run the app and your profile. If you ask us to delete your information (see below), we will delete what we can, except for records we are legally required to keep.

6. Your choices — access, correction, and deletion

You can ask us to show you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. To make a request, email 559rocn@gmail.com with the subject line “Privacy request” and tell us what you need. Include your anonymous profile ID if you know it. We will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Children’s privacy

ColorMatch Pro is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

8. Do Not Track

Our sites do not currently respond to “Do Not Track” browser signals, because we do not track you across websites in the first place. We run no advertising trackers, no analytics pixels, and no session-replay tools.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page and post the new version here. If a change affects how we handle information you have already given us, we will say so in the updated policy. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this policy or about your information, contact us at 559rocn@gmail.com.

A note on scope: This policy is written for a small sole-proprietor business in California. It is not a substitute for legal advice. If you have questions about your rights under California law, contact the California Attorney General’s office or a California attorney.
